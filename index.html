Dear Mohamed,

What you've been driving at BBK since joining speaks for itself. It isn't yet showing up where the market forms its view of the bank.

I tracked BBK against five competitors on Instagram across their most recent posts. BBK holds the second-largest audience in Bahrain retail banking but captures a smaller share of the market's attention than that position warrants. The gap is one of positioning rather than spending — and it is measurable.

One finding sits close to your remit: community and purpose-led content is the strongest-performing theme in this market by a clear margin, and it is nearly absent from BBK's own feed.

What follows is a short, honest read on where BBK stands against the field, and what the others are doing differently. I hope it's useful as the bank considers its next chapter.

Naheed

This is one platform.

Everything above comes from Instagram — six accounts, sixty posts, refreshed automatically twice a day. The same method extends to the places customers actually form an opinion:

  • — The BBK app, walked end to end — onboarding, transfers, card controls and support, set beside the same journeys on rival apps, with every point of friction ranked by what it costs you
  • — App store reviews — what customers say about the BBK app next to every competitor's, grouped by what they are actually complaining about
  • — Google reviews, branch by branch — where service perception is won and lost
  • — The website — how the digital front door compares to the field
  • — Competitor ad spend — what rivals are putting behind paid media, through Meta's ad library
  • — The other platforms — LinkedIn, X and TikTok, where the audiences differ

Each is a view of the market BBK doesn't have today. Built properly, they keep themselves up to date.

Happy to show you what any of them would look like.
